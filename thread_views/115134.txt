TITLE: logging: bound source_id without relying on assertions

*--- opening post by nashif (2026-08-01 10:54:57+00:00)
filter_get() and filter_set() bounded source_id with __ASSERT_NO_MSG alone,
which compiles out at CONFIG_ASSERT=n. The multidomain backend passes a
source_id straight out of an IPC message from another domain, which then
indexes the dynamic filter table.
