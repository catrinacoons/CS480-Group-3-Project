*--- Copilot (2026-08-19 13:06:20+00:00)
This overlay enables and uses both ADC1 and ADC2 (io-channels mixes &adc1 and &adc2, and &adc2 is set to status = "okay"). On STM32G4, the SoC devicetree explicitly documents that the two ADC instances cannot be used in parallel right now (see dts/arm/st/g4/stm32g4.dtsi around the adc1/adc2 definitions). This is likely to break at build- or run-time when the sample tries to access channels from both instances.

Consider constraining the sample overlay to a single ADC instance (e.g., route A2 to ADC1 as well if the pin/channel mapping allows), or otherwise split the configuration so only one ADC instance is enabled/used at a time.
