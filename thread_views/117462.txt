TITLE: net: ethernet: handle forwarded packets without headroom

*--- opening post by maass-hamburg (2026-08-26 12:03:11+00:00)
Packets allocated by another L2 do not have the Ethernet header
headroom reserved by ethernet_l2_alloc().
Use a separate header fragment when a forwarded packet cannot
accommodate the reserved Ethernet header in its first fragment.
