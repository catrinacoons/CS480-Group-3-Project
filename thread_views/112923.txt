tests: mem_protect: userspace: enable switching tests on Infineon boards

CONFIG_USERSPACE_SWITCHING_TESTS is opt-in and, per its Kconfig help text,
must be turned on through a board overlay. Add overlays enabling it for
the Infineon PSOC Control PSC3 (kit_psc3m5_evk) and the PSOC Edge PSE84
Cortex-M55 targets (kit_pse84_eval, kit_pse84_ai). These parts support
userspace and pass the thread switching tests; without the overlay the
user_only_switching, kernel_only_switching and kernel_user_mix_switching
subtests silently skip on these boards.

Verified on hardware: the three switching cases pass on kit_psc3m5_evk and
kit_pse84_eval/pse846gps2dbzc4a/m55.
