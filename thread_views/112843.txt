CONFIG_OPENAMP_RSC_TABLE is not enabled. AM62x M4 boards typically require the OpenAMP resource table for Linux remoteproc to boot the firmware; without it, remoteproc/RPMsg may fail to start even though CONFIG_OPENAMP is enabled.
