TITLE: drivers: i2c: rcar: use inclusive terminology

*--- opening post by kartben (2026-08-23 14:59:42+00:00)
Update comments and driver-local identifiers to use the controller/target terminology ratified by coding guideline A.2 and already used by the Zephyr I2C API, including renaming the driver-composed RCAR_I2C_ICMCR_MASTER convenience mask to RCAR_I2C_ICMCR_CONTROLLER.

Register mnemonics mirroring the R-Car datasheet (ICMCR, ICSCR, MNR, MAL, ...) and the comments spelling out their datasheet names are kept unchanged.
