TITLE: drivers: clock_monitor: add NXP ANACTRL freq-measure back-end

*--- opening post by FelixWang47831 (2026-07-17 09:11:38+00:00)
Add a clock_monitor back-end for the frequency-measurement feature of the NXP ANACTRL module. ANACTRL measures a target clock against a reference clock; both are routed through INPUTMUX and selected from devicetree "reference-sources" / "target-sources" child nodes.
    
ANACTRL has no completion interrupt, so a one-shot MEASURE is kicked in start() and polled from a k_work_delayable; a stuck target clock is bounded by a retry budget and reported as CLOCK_LOST. Only MEASURE mode is supported.
