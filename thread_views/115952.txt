TITLE: ethernet: nxp_enet_qos: align devicetree with other snps,dwmac controllers

*--- opening post by tpambor (2026-08-11 20:34:04+00:00)
The `nxp,enet-qos` ethernet controller used a two-node hierarchy (parent `nxp,enet-qos` for clocks + child `nxp,enet-qos-mac` for interrupts/MAC), which is inconsistent with other `snps,dwmac` controllers like MCX E series (https://github.com/zephyrproject-rtos/zephyr/pull/115755), [ESP32 ](https://github.com/zephyrproject-rtos/zephyr/blob/bbb2da98a3a83a40e42f713c46c653d4a57e0c28/dts/riscv/espressif/esp32p4/esp32p4_common.dtsi#L677-L697) or [STM32](https://github.com/zephyrproject-rtos/zephyr/blob/bbb2da98a3a83a40e42f713c46c653d4a57e0c28/dts/arm/st/h7/stm32h7.dtsi#L1231-L1252). This PR collapses it into a single `nxp,enet-qos` node.

Tested with `sample.net.ptp` on `frdm_mcxn947/mcxn947/cpu0`
