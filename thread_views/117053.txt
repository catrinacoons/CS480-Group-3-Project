*--- Copilot (2026-08-21 14:25:07+00:00)
Use the parsed MCC `len` here instead of `buf->len`. The latter includes any bytes beyond the TEST payload, so malformed frames echo unintended trailing data; it can also be 128–255, which `BT_RFCOMM_SET_LEN_8()` cannot encode as a valid one-byte length. Passing `len` keeps the response within the declared 7-bit MCC payload.
