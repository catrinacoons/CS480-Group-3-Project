Update comments and driver-local identifiers to use the controller/target terminology ratified by coding guideline A.2 and already used by the Zephyr I2C API.

Identifiers mirroring the DesignWare databook (e.g. IC_CON union bitfields, IC_TAR union bitfield ic_10bitaddr_master, IC_TX_ABRT_SOURCE union bitfields) are kept unchanged.
