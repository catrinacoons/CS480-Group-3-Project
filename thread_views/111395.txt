*--- opening post by d3zd3z (2026-06-16 14:03:17+00:00)
The vulnerabilities page had grown to over 2400 lines covering every CVE since
2017, making the current year's entries hard to find. This change moves each
previous year (2017, 2019–2025) into its own page under
`doc/security/vulnerabilities/` and links to them from a toctree at the top of
the main page, which now shows only the current year (2026).

It also gives the 2026 entries a proper CVE-2026 section heading; they had
previously been listed under the CVE-2025 header.

This is a docs-only reorganization with no content changes to the individual
CVE entries. It is split out from the CVE-disclosure work into its own PR
because the large file move would otherwise trip the compliance/size review.
