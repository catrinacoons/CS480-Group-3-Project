*--- Copilot (2026-06-11 10:52:04+00:00)
`diff` is stored in a `uint32_t`, but sequence numbers and `replay_counter` are 64-bit. Truncating the subtraction can mis-handle large sequence jumps (e.g. > 2^32), keeping stale bits in the replay window and causing incorrect accept/reject decisions. Use a 64-bit `diff` to preserve the full delta before comparing it to the replay window size.
