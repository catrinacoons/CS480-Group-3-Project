*--- opening post by Thalley (2026-09-07 14:50:12+00:00)
The prj.conf attempted to configure the number of ASEs when BT_ASCS=n.

Moved the UNICAST specific values from prj.conf to the Kconfig file and used configdefault to configure them instead. For this to have a proper effect, the peer_config struct was also modified so that unicast audio specific fields are guarded by CONFIG_BT_BAP_UNICAST_SERVER.

*--- Copilot (2026-09-07 14:53:36+00:00)
The broadcast-only configuration enabled by this branch still advertises `BT_UUID_ASCS_VAL` unconditionally in `main.c`'s `ad` array, even though `SAMPLE_UNICAST=n` leaves ASCS disabled. This makes the device claim a service it does not expose; gate the ASCS UUID on `CONFIG_BT_BAP_UNICAST_SERVER` just like the ASCS service-data entry.
