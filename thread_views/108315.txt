*--- Copilot (2026-05-04 08:25:25+00:00)
`connected_cb()` now unrefs `peer_conn` on failure without verifying that `peer_conn` is non-NULL and corresponds to the `conn` passed into the callback. Since `bt_conn_unref()` asserts on NULL, this can crash if the callback is invoked for a connection other than the one stored in `peer_conn` (similar to why `disconnected_cb()` guards with `if (conn != peer_conn) return;`). Prefer unref’ing `conn` directly on error and only clearing/unref’ing `peer_conn` when `conn == peer_conn`.
